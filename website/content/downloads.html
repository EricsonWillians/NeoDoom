Release builds are published on the GitHub Releases page. Every artifact has a matching .sha256 checksum file. A supported IWAD is required and is not included.

Current release: 4.15.8 — huge maps

  • Map coordinates and sector heights through ±262144, end to end (double-precision nodes, blockmap, UDMF validation).
  • Fog, visibility, and sky rendering scale to enormous maps; classic maps are unaffected.
  • Procedural map sizes up to 160.
