# BiasedDoom 4.15.8

BiasedDoom 4.15.8 is a huge-map release. The engine's coordinate ceiling rises
from the ±32768 of 16.16 fixed point to ±262144 (`MAX_MAP_COORD`) end to end,
sector floor and ceiling heights follow through UDMF, and the fog, sky, and
procedural systems scale to maps that would previously have rendered as a
black wall of distance-clamped geometry.

## Huge Map Coordinates

- Map coordinates and sector heights are accepted through ±262144 everywhere:
  double-precision node building and traversal, `node_t` partition lines and
  bounding boxes, subsector and blockmap lookups, and UDMF validation.
- UDMF `heightfloor`/`heightceiling` values may reach the full range, so
  floors and ceilings can sit far beyond the classic ±32767 int16 limit of
  the binary map formats. Heresy Editor 2.5.0 authors these maps directly.
- Legacy GL node formats that cannot represent oversized maps are rejected
  and rebuilt in double precision automatically.
- The hardware far plane reaches 262144 units, and sky walls, portal stencil
  caps, and horizon grids scale to `HW_SKY_EXTENT` so tall sky sectors never
  show their edges.

## Visibility and Fog at Scale

- Fog density, global visibility, thick-fog distance, and the sky fog veil
  scale automatically with the map's bounding-box diagonal, so distant
  geometry on huge maps is no longer crushed to black by constants tuned for
  ~2000-unit maps. Classic-size maps are completely unaffected.
- Fog shading is rewritten around exponential optical-depth transmittance
  with optional spatial turbulence, banding dither, and a smooth
  minimum-visibility floor without contour discontinuities.
- The sky dome renders its own atmospheric horizon layer with per-vertex
  alpha and a true pole, so translucent fog passes never double-blend and
  never expose fan seams.

## Sky Rendering

- The zenith cap now renders a per-wedge color gradient sampled from the sky
  texture's own edge bands, replacing the flat average-color disc that filled
  the view when looking up inside a very tall sky sector.
- The sky renders at full brightness in every light mode; it no longer
  inherits stale sector light levels.
- Fixed uninitialized lightmap coordinates on sky vertices and a red/blue
  channel inversion on the zenith cap gradient.

## Procedural Generation

- Procedural map size now ranges to 160 (from 80) with a widened coordinate
  safety band, taking advantage of the extended coordinate range.

## Quality Assurance

- Fixed a software-renderer visplane hash that overflowed fixed point on
  plane heights beyond ±32767.
- Verified in-engine: UDMF sectors with floors at −100000 and ceilings at
  +100000 render, spawn, and simulate correctly.
