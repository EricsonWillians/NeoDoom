Examples — twenty-five playable modes

Every example is a self-contained directory under examples/python/ — drop one on the engine with -file and it just runs. No build step, no PK3 packing, no ZScript.

./biaseddoom -iwad ~/games/doom2.wad -python \
    -file examples/python/24_wave_defense +map map01

# or browse interactively (curses launcher with headless test mode):
tools/play-python-example.py

The suite is also the test suite: tools/test-python-examples.sh --iwad /path/to/doom2.wad compiles, packages, and smoke-runs every example in the real engine.

Featured: 24_wave_defense — endless horde mode

An endless survival mode that commandeers any map: it captures the map's own monster spawn points (nothing ever spawns in a wall), retires sealed teleport closets by itself, and escalates forever — Diablo-2-style affixes with real mechanics, hand-crafted named uniques, per-wave mutators, a weapon ladder with arsenal-aware loot, twin bosses, and distance-adaptive health bars that never lie.
