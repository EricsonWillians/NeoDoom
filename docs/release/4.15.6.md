# BiasedDoom 4.15.6

BiasedDoom 4.15.6 is a procedural spatial-composition, environmental, audio,
and compatibility release. Generated missions now use a clear hierarchy of
connectors, small rooms, medium combat spaces, and major compound landmarks;
liquids shape whole regions; opportunity reveals and elevated monster positions
have multiple architectural families; and successful short ACS calls no longer
flood the normal console.

## Highlights

- Replaced the repeated near-square room rhythm with explicit connector, small,
  medium, and major spatial classes plus compact, horizontal, vertical, and
  compound/bent growth families. L-, T-, cross-, stepped, axial, and asymmetric
  rooms now coexist in one deterministic map.
- Added seed-stable 368/384/400-unit row and column cadence, twelve chamber
  proportion profiles, asymmetric and non-45-degree shoulders, broader
  same-room joins, longer same-stage foldback loops, and raised cross-room
  sightline windows.
- Promoted water, blood, nukage, and lava into macro architecture: whole flooded
  rooms with dry islands, irregular reservoirs, trenches, paired basins, and
  straight, staggered, or bending multi-cell watercourses with 64-unit dry
  causeways and protected circulation.
- Expanded key ambushes and switch opportunities into freestanding pavilions,
  framed wall alcoves, and verified perimeter false-wall chambers. Moving faces
  can be prominent, subtly framed, or visually matched to an ordinary wall and
  concealed on the automap.
- Expanded elevated ranged positions into stair platforms, chamfered turrets,
  and wall-backed balconies with straight, offset, or dogleg 16-unit stair
  approaches.
- Added deterministic IWAD soundtrack selection and corrected its level-load
  lifecycle so the chosen track actually starts without advancing layout RNG.

## Console and Audio Fixes

Brutal Doom and similar mods can synchronously query short ACS functions from
weapon states. Successful termination was previously printed as a normal
notification even though script start messages were developer-level trace data.
Successful completion now uses the same trace channel. Warnings, invalid module
messages, unknown calls, VM failures, and other diagnostics remain unchanged.

OpenAL per-source Doppler is now assigned only when
`AL_EXT_source_distance_model` exists. This removes repeated `AL_INVALID_ENUM`
errors on affected implementations and restores FluidSynth, TiMidity++, OPL,
GUS, WildMIDI, ADL, and OPN streaming there.

## Procedural Safety and Compatibility

- Fluid, reveal, perch, lift, key, exit, actor, pickup, and mandatory-passage
  reservations are resolved before geometry emission.
- Nukage retains 5 Slime damage every 32 tics. Lava retains 5 Fire damage every
  16 tics, radiation-suit leakage, and terrain effects. Water and blood remain
  harmless.
- Hazardous watercourses retain a wholly dry route, flooded rooms retain a dry
  actor/reward island, and no initial actor or pickup is emitted into liquid.
- Sightline windows never add a traversable progression edge. Reveal chambers,
  doors, monster routes, stairs, and false-wall void reservations are validated
  from serialized UDMF rather than inferred from the requested template.
- Doom, Ultimate Doom, and Doom II use only flats, textures, music markers, and
  actor vocabularies available in their active IWAD.

## Release Validation

The release candidate passed a clean engine build and the complete procedural
regression set:

- representative structural validation from size 1 through 80;
- byte-identical determinism and eleven additional rotating seed/settings cases;
- all eight liquid architectures, four liquid flats, three reveal families,
  three reveal cues, three perch families, and three stair approaches;
- five-theme structural differentiation and all four style-control effects;
- native door profiles, secret reward progression, difficulty scaling, and
  sizes 1/3/5/20/80 runtime loading;
- all five themes under Ultimate Doom and all five themes at size 80 through the
  real node builder;
- five unrelated maximum-size BSP/node stress maps;
- a 15.65 MB all-high size-80 map with 7,940 sectors and 8,432 things.

The final determinism fingerprint is:

```text
eb02baefb951a8785eb40137709b0347b0982d06f49e3b8182a33cb31068912c
```

A ten-minute Brutal Doom MAP01 interaction soak repeatedly moved, fired,
switched weapons, and toggled the automap. Developer level 3 produced zero
`GetCrosshair` lifecycle messages while retaining 193 legitimate script
diagnostics. A developer-level-4 control produced 149 matching starts and
completions in 30 seconds, proving the query still executed normally.

## Downloads

- Linux: download `BiasedDoom-4.15.6-Linux-x86_64.AppImage`, mark it executable
  if needed, and run it.
- Windows x64: extract `BiasedDoom-4.15.6-Windows-x64.zip` and run
  `biaseddoom.exe`.
- Windows x64 MinGW: extract
  `BiasedDoom-4.15.6-Windows-x64-MinGW.zip` for the Linux-built cross-compiled
  package.
- macOS 10.15 or newer: extract `BiasedDoom-4.15.6-macOS.tar.gz` and launch the
  application.
- Every artifact has a matching `.sha256` checksum file.
- A supported IWAD such as `DOOM2.WAD` is required and is not included.
